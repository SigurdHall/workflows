# Envelope

The machine-readable result of any step, and the only thing that crosses
a step boundary. Workers, synthesizers, reviewers, gates, and flows all
speak envelope — nothing downstream parses prose.

## Core fields

Shared by all envelope types (published as schema `$defs` for reuse):

- `schema_version` — a `const` per schema; unversioned data is invalid.
- identity — `*_id`, `*_revision`, and candidate identity as
  `{id, digest, immutable}` with `sha256:`-prefixed digests. Provenance
  (git commit, artifact store) is pluggable, not baked in.
- `status` — `COMPLETED | FAILED | BLOCKED | STOPPED`, plus `terminal`.
- results — `criterion_results[]` with
  `PASS | FAIL | NOT_RUN | INCONCLUSIVE`; `findings[]` with severity
  `CRITICAL | HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW`, claim, evidence refs, and the
  smallest required action.
- `evidence_refs[]` — every claim points at evidence; a claim without a
  logged probe is an assertion, not a finding.
- `non_claims[]` — required, minimum one: what this envelope explicitly
  does *not* assert (e.g. "benchmark evidence only; no release
  approval"). A PASS is bounded by its non-claims.
- `side_effects[]` — what the step touched, ideally "nothing".

## Rules

- Schema-validated at every boundary (a gate, see `gate.md`). The
  motivating experiments lost a coordination round to hand-written
  free-text envelopes — twice.
- Envelopes are generated by the driver from structured step output,
  never hand-authored by a model in free text.
- Negative-path claims ("invalid transitions are rejected") require a
  logged probe in `evidence_refs`. This rule exists because a reviewer in
  the motivating experiments asserted exactly that property, unprobed, on
  the one candidate where it was false.
- Scores and rubrics are *not* core fields. Verdicts carry criterion
  results and findings; consumers that want a numeric rubric extend the
  schema. Score averages reward lenient reviewers — measure reviewers on
  reproducible findings instead.

## Anti-patterns

- Prose summaries as the interface between steps.
- "PASS, 100/100" with empty evidence and no non-claims.
- Extending core schemas with domain fields instead of composing them.
